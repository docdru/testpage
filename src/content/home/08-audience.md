## Who is FocusWatch for?

FocusWatch is aimed at

- **Families** with children who have ADHD or a general focus and attention deficit, and who are looking for tools to support the child's organization, attention and self-regulation together.
- **Educators and therapists** who want to extend their methods beyond the therapy or lesson hour, and give children a tool for the rest of their daily life.
