A smartwatch-based learning and everyday aid for **children with ADHD**, built to support
self-regulation, organization and attention in daily life.

- Support with starting, staying on task and structuring
- Subtle cues, exactly when they are needed
- Developed together with therapists, schools and research (ZHAW)
