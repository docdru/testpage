## Scientifically grounded, developed close to practice

FocusWatch is based on current research findings on ADHD, self-regulation and digital
health support.

Studies show that children benefit most from **timely, context-aware cues**, support
exactly in the moment of challenge. Digital aids are most effective when they are close to
everyday life, unobtrusive and individually adjustable.

**Tryon et al. (2006)** demonstrate that direct feedback can improve behavior in the
classroom. In their study children received vibration feedback based on their activity.
The result: in 7 of 9 children, hyperactivity scores fell by 20 to 47% compared to
baseline.

**Domsch et al. (2022)** confirm that educators consider interventions for ADHD effective,
yet they are often hard to implement in practice. Lack of time, group size and workload are
cited as the main barriers, underlining the need for supportive, scalable tools.

FocusWatch is developed in close collaboration with:

- Researchers at ZHAW
- Occupational therapists
- Teachers and schools
- Affected families
