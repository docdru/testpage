### What we do

We share knowledge, bring people together and develop solutions together.

- **Inform**
  Together with ZHAW and partners, we organise talks and webinars for parents, teachers and
  therapists: which digital tools make sense, for which child, and how to use them well?
- **Connect and co-create**
  Parents, children and professionals help shape our work in workshops. A sounding board of parents
  and professionals gives us critical feedback.
- **Develop and test**
  With FocusWatch, we are developing a concrete tool for everyday life and testing it with families,
  occupational therapists and schools.
