### What makes FocusWatch different

- **In the moment**
  The watch responds when attention drifts, not afterwards.
- **Made for children**
  Designed for 8- to 14-year-olds, with a parent app for settings and an overview.
- **Developed with occupational therapy**
  Rooted in occupational therapy practice, so support continues at home.
