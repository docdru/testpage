### Our FocusWatch studies

- #### 51 children

  **Classroom study, 2025**
  4 classes, with a placebo comparison. Writing performance and focus time tended to develop better
  (not all effects significant). Children rated FocusWatch as helpful more often than anything else.
- #### 12 interviews

  **Needs of families and professionals, 2025**
  Parents, children, occupational therapists, teachers and a paediatrician confirm the gap in
  everyday life. Checklists and vibration were particularly appreciated.
- #### 6 occupational therapists

  **Use in therapy, 2026**
  Master's thesis at ZHAW: high acceptance among children, better structure with checklists, colour
  breathing helps with regulation.
- #### 7 out of 7

  **occupational therapists would recommend FocusWatch, 2026**
  Survey as part of an Innosuisse feasibility study. Stigma is the biggest hurdle; the discreet
  design helps.
