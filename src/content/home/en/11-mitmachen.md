## Be part of AttentionLab.

AttentionLab is for parents, professionals and anyone who wants to support children with attention
difficulties.

- **Become a member**
  Sign up to join the conversation and stay informed about news and events. Annual fee
  ==[CHF, decided by the general assembly]==
- **Become a user**
  Test FocusWatch and help us develop better solutions with your feedback.
- **Support us**
  There are many ways to support AttentionLab, from a small contribution to an active role in the
  team. Choose what suits you.

  <details>
  <summary>Ways to support us</summary>

  - **Patron / supporting member:**
    financial or non-financial support ==[from CHF]==
  - **Volunteer:**
    at events, test rounds or simply by spreading the word
  - **A role in the association:**
    We are looking for support in two areas:
    - **Community management:**
      supporting families and test rounds, organising events
    - **Development & IT:**
      maintaining the app, iOS updates, new features (Swift/watchOS)

    Voluntary or paid ==[depending on funding]==. Projects and theses are possible for students.

  </details>
