## Research: does feedback at the right moment help?

Research shows that real-time feedback can work. But there are hardly any solutions made for
children that can be used in everyday life.

- #### 20–47 %

  **Feedback on the body can work**
  Vibration and light signals worn on the body reduced hyperactivity in children with ADHD.
  Tryon et al., 2006 (9 children).
- **Schools need support they can put into practice**
  Teachers often find effective measures hard to implement. What is missing most is one-to-one
  support and ongoing contact with parents. Domsch et al., 2022 (196 school staff).
- **There are hardly any ready-to-use solutions for children**
  Our review of sensor-based tools: only around 10 % of the solutions are ready for use, and most
  give feedback through the teacher rather than directly to the child. In preparation, preliminary
  results.
- **An international topic too ==[for discussion: list yes/no]==**
  Revibe (Pearson, USA, 2025) also uses vibration reminders on a smartwatch, with a focus on adults
  and the US market.
