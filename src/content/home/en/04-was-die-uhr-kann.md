### What the watch does

- **Notice.** Motion sensors detect when writing stops.
- **Remind.** A gentle vibration helps the child return to the task. Nobody else hears a thing.
- **Structure.** Routines and tasks for the morning, homework and the evening, step by step.
- **Regulate.** Colour breathing and a digital fidget spinner for difficult moments.
- **Focus timer.** Split work into short, manageable rounds.
- **Motivate.** Marbles and badges for successes. Parents see progress in a dashboard.
