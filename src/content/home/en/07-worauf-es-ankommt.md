### What matters

In 2026, around ten families tested FocusWatch in everyday life over several weeks. This is what we learned:

- **Start with routines.** Checklists for the morning and evening were used the most.
- **Set it up together.** Setting up takes time at first. Plan it together with your child.
- **Charging is part of the routine.** The biggest hurdle: a flat battery or a forgotten watch.
- **Involve the teacher.** Many children also wore the watch at school.
- **Let the child decide.** The watch works when the child wants it.
