## The vision

We believe children with ADHD need more than occasional support in daily life. They need
continuous, everyday companionship that helps them work with their own abilities, while
learning, working and growing.

The focus is not on control, but on **self-efficacy**. Through subtle cues, clear structure
and positive feedback, FocusWatch aims to help children build their self-regulation and
attention control step by step.

In the long term we see FocusWatch as part of an integrated support ecosystem:
complementing today's therapeutic approaches and developed together with research,
therapists, schools and families.
