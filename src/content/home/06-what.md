## What is FocusWatch?

FocusWatch is an Apple Watch app that accompanies children through their day and supports
focus, structure and self-regulation, **without constant screen use**.

While existing digital solutions mostly offer organizational help, FocusWatch goes a step
further: using sensor data it detects, for example, when writing is interrupted, and gives
a gentle vibration reminder to refocus on the task.

The app combines proven pedagogical and therapeutic principles with modern technology.

### Core features

- **Structuring:** checklists and daily overview
- **Starting:** gentle vibration cues
- **Staying on task:** focus and writing support
- **Regulating:** short breathing and calming exercises
- **Motivating:** positive feedback and rewards

The first target group is primary-school-age children who are learning self-organization,
focus and writing. Adults, particularly parents and teachers, can configure the watch
through a smartphone app and monitor how effective the interventions are. The solution is
based on a commercial smartwatch running a purpose-built app. Because of its high market
share and the existing infrastructure in schools, the Apple Watch with iPhone integration
is used as the platform.

> FocusWatch is not a replacement for therapy or medication, it is complementary,
> everyday support.
