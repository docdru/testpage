## Where is the project today?

FocusWatch is an ongoing research and development project with academic roots at ZHAW.

- Working prototype (Apple Watch)
- Qualitative studies with children, parents, therapists and teachers

The project is currently funded by Innosuisse and is preparing an extended testing and
validation phase.

### Interested in FocusWatch?

We would love to hear from:

- Families who want to learn more
- Therapists and teachers
- Schools and institutions
- Research partners

If you are interested in joining a future study or collaborating with us, please get in touch.
